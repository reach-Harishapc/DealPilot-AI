## 2. 7-Minute Demo Script (Minute-by-Minute)

* **Minute 0:00 – 1:00 | The Hook & Problem (Slide):**
  - *"Enterprise sales reps spend 3-4 hours before every major client meeting digging through emails, LinkedIn, 10-Ks, and product brochures. What happens? Reps walk in with generic slides, feature-dump, and lose high-value deals. Meet DealPilot AI."*
* **Minute 1:00 – 2:30 | The Meeting Command Center:**
  - Open DealPilot AI dashboard.
  - Show upcoming calendar meetings (e.g., *"Maersk Logistics - VP of Supply Chain"*).
  - Click **"Generate Battlecard"** live.
* **Minute 2:30 – 4:00 | Deep-Dive into the Generated Battlecard:**
  - **Account Dossier:** Pain points, industry pressures, recent signals.
  - **Persona Intelligence:** Buyer motivations, fears, personal KPIs.
  - **Custom Value Proposition:** Showing how our enterprise solution directly answers their problem.
  - **Golden Discovery Questions:** 3 targeted questions that make the rep look like a seasoned consultant.
* **Minute 4:00 – 5:15 | Dynamic Judge Challenge (Prove It's Not Hardcoded!):**
  - Invite the judge: *"Give us any company and executive title right now."*
  - Type it into the **Custom Meeting Analyzer**, watch it synthesize in real-time.
* **Minute 5:15 – 6:15 | Interactive Objection Simulator:**
  - Test a tough customer pushback: *"Your pricing is 40% higher than Competitor X."*
  - DealPilot generates instant tactical rebuttal techniques tailored to the persona.
* **Minute 6:15 – 7:00 | What We Would Build Next & Wrap-up:**
  - Highlight future roadmap (Live call whisperer, automated CRM sync, cross-BU collision detection).

## 3. Anticipated Judge Q&A (3 Minutes)

1. **Q: How do you avoid hallucinations in high-stakes sales meetings?**
   - *A:* We ground the AI with strict system instructions, structured JSON schemas, and context injection from verified sources (internal CRM data + verified company records). If data is absent, the AI explicitly highlights assumptions rather than fabricating facts.
2. **Q: How does this scale for an enterprise with 50 different products?**
   - *A:* We implement semantic retrieval over the product catalog to match only the relevant modules to the customer's identified pain points, avoiding the classic "catalog dump."
3. **Q: How is this different from simply using ChatGPT?**
   - *A:* ChatGPT lacks organizational context (your CRM history, your product catalog, multi-BU awareness) and doesn't structure outputs into actionable sales methodologies (BANT, MEDDIC, objection rebuttals).
