# 01. Problem Framing & Opportunity Selection

## 1. Problem Statement Decomposition
The problem brief focuses on large B2B enterprises selling complex portfolios (multiple products, services, BUs, and regions). 
In this environment, sales professionals face high friction and cognitive overload:

| Core Question from Brief | Root Cause | Impact on Business |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"How can sales teams prepare faster for meetings, follow-ups, proposals?"** | Context is scattered across CRM notes, news, emails, annual reports, product collateral. Reps spend 2–4 hours per meeting. | Reps walk into meetings underprepared, pitches sound generic, discovery fails. |
| **"Which accounts or leads should I prioritize, and why?"** | Lack of real-time signal synthesis (buying triggers, executive changes, pain points). | Wasted quota chasing cold leads; missed warm windows. |
| **"What is happening inside an account that creates opportunity or risk?"** | Information silos; news & earnings not connected to product catalog. | Competitor displacement happens unnoticed; churn risks ignored. |
| **"How do I customize my company's pitches to customer challenges?"** | Too many product features, hard to map prospect's specific pain point to the exact solution module. | "Feature dumping" instead of value selling. |
| **"Single source of truth across BUs/regions serving a customer?"** | Multiple teams operating in isolation without unified account visibility. | Account fatigue, internal collision, lost cross-sell revenue. |

## 3. The Selected Direction: "DealPilot AI" (Pre-Meeting Intelligence & Dynamic Sales Copilot)

### The Problem in One Sentence:
> *"Enterprise B2B account executives spend over 35% of their working hours manually gathering fragmented data before prospect meetings—resulting in surface-level research, generic slide decks, and missed high-value deal conversions."*

### Why This Wins:
1. **Relatable & Immediate:** Every judge immediately understands the pain of an uninspired, generic sales pitch.
2. **Interactive Live Demo:** A judge can challenge the system during Q&A: *"Can you run this for Acme Corp meeting with the Chief Risk Officer?"* and DealPilot will dynamically formulate a real-time account briefing and custom value proposition.
3. **Strict Compliance with Rules:** Genuinely dynamic, powered by LLM synthesis + structured synthetic CRM data + search intelligence.
