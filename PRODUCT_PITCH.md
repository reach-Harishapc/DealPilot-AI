# DealPilot Enterprise by Rish AI Labs — Executive Product Brief & Pitch

> **Autonomous Pre-Meeting Intelligence & Multi-BU Deal Strategy Engine**  
> *Developed by Rish AI Labs — Transforming fragmented account signals into winning, executive-ready sales conversations.*

---

## 1. The "WHY" (The Core Enterprise Problem)

In large multi-product B2B enterprises (selling across AI, Cloud, Cybersecurity, and Supply Chain):
- **Enterprise Account Executives (AEs) spend 4–6 hours per meeting** manually cobbling together notes from 10-K filings, LinkedIn profiles, and disparate CRM records.
- **Pitches remain generic and single-threaded**: AEs pitch whatever product they know best, missing multi-million dollar cross-sell opportunities across other Business Units (BUs).
- **Meetings stall on predictable objections**: When a CFO or VP raises a budget freeze or "we can build this in-house," AEs lack the psychological counter-strategies (MEDDIC / Challenger Sale) to pivot the conversation, resulting in elongated deal cycles and slipping quotas.

---

## 2. The "WHAT" (Product Definition)

**DealPilot Enterprise** is an autonomous pre-meeting intelligence platform. It continuously scans upcoming executive calendar calls, matches target accounts against a multi-BU enterprise solutions catalog, and synthesizes a **4-part strategic meeting brief** before the AE enters the room:
1. **Strategic Value Proposition & Pitch Track**: Tailored 2-minute executive elevator pitch and ROI projections.
2. **Consultative Discovery Playbook**: High-leverage probing questions mapped to current company market triggers.
3. **Objection Battlecards**: Psychological buyer root-cause analysis and verbatim consultative counter-tracks.
4. **Executive Email Draft**: 1-click personalized pre-meeting / follow-up email.

## 4. The "HOW" (How the Engine Operates)

```
[ HubSpot CRM & Calendar ] ──► Live meetings, deals & contacts from Portal 247526396
          │
          ▼
[ Account & Signal Engine ] ──► Ingests 10-K filings, news triggers, CRM health & active ARR
          │
          ▼
[ Multi-BU Solution Match ] ──► Scores & maps solutions across AI, Cloud, Cyber & Supply Chain
          │
          ▼
[ Google Gemini AI Engine ] ──► Synthesizes tailored pitch, stakeholder psychology & discovery tracks
          │
          ▼
[ AE Cockpit & Coach AI ]   ──► Delivers 4-tab interactive brief + live interactive objection simulator
```

1. **Single Unified Hub (HubSpot CRM & Meetings)**: Ingests scheduled calls, opportunities, and buyer attendee profiles directly from HubSpot Portal 247526396—eliminating external calendar and meeting tool fragmentation.
2. **Autonomous Signal Ingestion**: Contextualizes account revenue, current contracts, and live market signals (e.g. supply chain rerouting, regulatory audits).
3. **Deterministic Cross-BU Alignment**: Identifies which enterprise solution module has the highest strategic fit for that specific executive's KPIs.
4. **Generative Strategic Reasoning (Gemini Live)**: Formulates verbatim talking tracks, eliminating corporate buzzwords in favor of economic ROI reframes.
5. **Interactive Objection Simulator (Coach AI)**: Allows the AE to rehearse against pushbacks (budget freeze, competitors, timing) and receive real-time psychological coaching tips.
